How it Works

01

01. INITIAL VISIT

An initial visit typically helps identify pest activity and conditions at a home or office so that possible service approaches can be evaluated.

  • Property inspection
  • Advice on available options
  • Discussion of a potential service plan

02

02. SERVICES PROPOSAL

Following an inspection, a service provider may outline treatment methods, anticipated scheduling and costs based on the individual property.

  • Proposed treatment options
  • Scope and timing
  • Estimate for consideration

03. PROJECT EXECUTION

Once a plan is agreed, treatment may be performed according to the provider's process, with guidance on follow-up and prevention.

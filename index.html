Choosing a provider

What to Look For in Pest Control

Treatment precautions

Ask about precautions for children, pets and occupants.

Provider experience

Review the provider's relevant experience and reputation.

Scope of work

Look for a clear scope of work and follow-up procedures.

Treatment options

Treatment options should reflect individual property conditions.

Licensing and insurance

Verify applicable licenses and training directly with a provider.

Service availability

Confirm availability, response times and service coverage.

Requests we cannot accept

Animal control or wildlife removal, wasps, bees, bed bugs, rats, raccoons, foxes, bats, cats, chipmunks, squirrels, woodchucks, crane flies, drain flies, grasshoppers, fruit flies, gnats, honeybees, aphids, snails, grubs, slugs, chiggers, chinch bugs, lizards, maggots, mites (including scabies) and snakes. Commercial buildings and mobile homes are not eligible.
